VOID VIGIL

The Cinder Reach Crusade

The Cinder Reach is coming apart. Grakk warbands loot its edges, the iron Revenants march out of their ossuaries, and the Vorrid Swarm is gorging on its farming worlds. Worse, the Weeping Rift is widening at its heart, and the Ashen Choir sings to the fallen god inside it. You are a Vigil-Marshal of the Void Vigil, the ash-armoured crusaders sworn from eight great Houses. Lead your Cohort, cleanse the Reach, and silence the Choir.
